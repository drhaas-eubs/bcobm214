Core message · Bostelaar
The one thing to remember
"AI changes the speed and the inputs of investing — not the laws of risk and return."
Why it matters
Hype suggests AI repeals diversification and risk. Anchoring on timeless principles prevents costly over-belief.
What it covers
Portfolio principles, ML disruption of finance, prediction & risk tooling, robo-advice, and algo/HFT trading.
Convince · inspireClarify · instruct
Message strategy: Quantitative, decision-oriented audience → lean Clarify; convince only where AI hype must be tempered.
