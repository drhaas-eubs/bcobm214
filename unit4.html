Core message · Bostelaar
The one thing to remember
"A wrapper is a feature, not a moat — durable AI value is owned data, defensible IP and switching cost."
Why it matters
Investors fund moats, not demos. Students must tell a defensible asset from a thin layer over someone else's model.
What it covers
IP categories under AI, wrapper vs. moat, data as IP, authorship & licensing, and safeguarding innovation.
Convince · inspireClarify · instruct
Message strategy: Mixed legal/strategy content → balance: convince why moats matter, clarify the legal mechanics.
