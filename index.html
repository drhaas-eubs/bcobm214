Course Units

Ten teaching units across three phases — from the first idea to a funded, pitch-ready business plan. Each unit pairs a signature dynamic format — Presentation, Case Study, Role Play, Hackathon or Business Challenge — with a core message distilled in Kurt Bostelaar's method, its Intended Learning Outcome, a teaching case, a Thumbnail-Thinking framework gallery, pre-reading, a reflection prompt, and the framework authors. Expand a unit to begin.
