Reflection Prompt

Unit 5: Agility, Disruption & Rebranding in the Age of AI

Reflection · independent study

Complete these before the next session. Bring written responses — they open our next discussion. The goal is clarity, not certainty: collect distinctions and testable assumptions, not final answers.

Reflection 1

Which framework from today's Unit 5 Slibrary most changed how you see "agility & disruption"? Describe the exact moment of insight — what did you understand differently once the visual clicked?

Reflection 2

Take the unit's core message — "AI doesn't reward the biggest — it rewards the fastest to re-decide. Agility is the new moat." Do you agree? Argue the strongest case against it using at least one framework and one Giant from this unit.

Reflection 3

Pick one framework you do not yet fully understand. Return to the gallery, study its thumbnail, and write a 200-word explanation in your own words — then state one testable assumption it would help you check in a real organisation.
