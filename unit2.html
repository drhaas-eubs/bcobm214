Core message · Bostelaar
The one thing to remember
"AI value lags AI investment — benefits are real but arrive on a J-curve, not a switch."
Why it matters
Boards expect instant ROI and lose faith in the dip. Naming the lag protects good bets from being killed early.
What it covers
The productivity paradox, function-level benefits, automation gains, and revenue/retention measurement.
Convince · inspireClarify · instruct
Message strategy: Skeptical-but-engaged audience → balanced blend: convince that benefits are real, clarify how to measure them.
