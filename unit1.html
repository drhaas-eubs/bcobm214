Core message · Bostelaar
The one thing to remember
"Data is only an asset when it becomes a decision — AI turns customer signals into growth."
Why it matters
Most firms drown in data yet starve for insight. Growth comes from converting signals into decisions customers can feel.
What it covers
Decision pipelines, engagement loops, personalisation, embedded vs. AI-native product choices, and opportunity sensing.
