Session Guide — Unit 6

Investment Strategies in the AI Era · a 150-minute experiential session (12 × 2.5h course rhythm).

7-Phase Session · What to do today

EU Business School pedagogy: hands-on, scaffolded, higher-order. The lecture is minimal; the room does the thinking. Frameworks come from the Unit 6 Slibrary; pressure comes from the case.

Pedagogy: Live Data Lab  ·  Core message anchor: "AI changes the speed and the inputs of investing — not the laws of risk and return."
Open
0–10 min
Hook — Investment AI in the wild
Open with a 90-second provocation tied to "AI changes the speed and the inputs of investing — not the laws of risk and return.". Surface what students already believe before any framework is introduced.
Frame
10–25 min
Frame the core message
Reveal the unit's WHY → WHAT core message and place it on the Convince ↔ Clarify spectrum. Students predict where today's decision should sit.
Explore
25–50 min
Gallery walk — explore the Slibrary
In pairs, students browse the Unit 6 Framework Gallery and pick the three thumbnails that best fit today's case. Thumbnail Thinking in practice.
Apply
50–95 min
Live Data Lab — apply under pressure
Live Data Lab: hands-on with a no-code AI / spreadsheet model — audit the data, choose a metric, stress-test the result, and prepare a board-ready read-out.
Synthesise
95–120 min
Convene the Council of Giants
Groups run their recommendation past the unit's five Giants. Where do the Giants disagree? That friction is the insight.
Reflect
120–135 min
Reflect — distinguish, don't conclude
Each student names one distinction the session sharpened and one testable assumption they will carry forward (not a final answer).
Close
135–150 min
Close & bridge to Unit 7
One-sentence synthesis from each group, then a teaser and a single pre-reading task for next session.
Facilitator note
Keep students on the Convince ↔ Clarify spectrum: early phases build belief, later phases clarify mechanics. End on testable assumptions, never on a single "right" answer — clarity, not certainty.
